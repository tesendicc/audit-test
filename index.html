Our story

Riverbend Studio started in a converted garage in 2016. What began as a hobby turned into a small working studio, now run by two potters and a rotating cast of very supervisory cats.

We believe good pottery should be used, not just displayed — so everything we make is built for daily life: coffee in the morning, dinner at night, flowers on the table.

How each piece is made

  1. 1. Throwing

    Clay is centered and shaped on the wheel by hand.

  2. 2. Trimming

    Once leather-hard, each piece is trimmed to refine its final shape and foot.

  3. 3. Bisque firing

    Pieces are fired once to harden the clay before glazing.

  4. 4. Glazing & final firing

    Glaze is applied by hand, then fired a second time at a higher temperature.
